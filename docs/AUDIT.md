# What the old site was losing, and what the new one does

Written for Jarrett, November 9, 2026.

The old site was one page built on the franchisor's GoHighLevel account. Google knew exactly one URL, so you only showed up in the map pack. Anyone searching "bin cleaning Destin" or "trash can cleaning Navarre" landed on competitors. The email link on the page was broken, and every "Sign Up" sent people straight into a blank Jobber form with no price shown.

| Today (old site) | New site | Try it |
|---|---|---|
| One page for everything | A page for each of your 13 towns, plus pricing, FAQ, about and contact | [/service-areas](/service-areas) |
| "Sign Up" opens an empty Jobber form | ZIP check, plan, number of bins and exact price first, then your details, then Jobber | [/book](/book) |
| Rental owners and HOAs get the same form | Their own pages with a call-back form | [/vacation-rentals](/vacation-rentals), [/neighborhoods](/neighborhoods) |
| Questions mean a phone call | Ask button answers prices, areas and how it works, and hands anything else to you | Tap **Ask** in the bottom bar |
| Email link broken | Works everywhere; one place to change it | Footer |
| Nothing for Google or AI search to read | Business details (schema), sitemap and an llms.txt for AI answers | [/llms.txt](/llms.txt) |
| No way to ask for reviews | A short link for your follow-up texts and a card on the bin | [/review](/review) |
| Website only | Installs on a phone like an app and works offline (call/text buttons still work) | Open on your phone, then "Add to Home Screen" |

## What we need from you
1. Permission to use the Bin Busters logo, mascot and photos, or your own photos of the trailer and before/after bins.
2. Where web requests should go: an email address, a text, or straight into Jobber.
3. Anything special for multiple rentals, HOAs or commercial dumpsters.
4. Your Google sign-in for Search Console, Analytics and the Business Profile changes.

## Phasing
1. Go live on binbusters-emeraldcoast.com (two DNS records).
2. Search Console, Bing, Analytics; update the Business Profile category and website link.
3. Google Workspace email (jarrett@binbusters-emeraldcoast.com) and switch it everywhere.
4. Ask every customer for a review with /review. Rockstar Bin Cleaning has 214; you have 15.
